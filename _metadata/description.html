Always know which way you are looking. Compass shows the direction the camera is facing, either as text in the game bar or as a small compass strip on screen.

Features

  • Game bar (default): the direction next to Earnings, for example "Northwest", "Northwest 315°" or just "315°".
  • On-screen compass: a strip with N, NE, E and so on that scrolls as you turn the camera, with the direction you face highlighted. Place it top, middle or bottom, on the left or right side of the screen, in one of three sizes.
  • Stays clear of the game's own buttons, and clicks pass through it.
  • Adapts to the game's font size setting.
  • Purely cosmetic: it does not change your savegame, and achievements stay enabled.

Settings

Open the mod list when starting a new game or loading a savegame, and click the gear icon on Compass:

  • Compass position: Game bar, Top left, Top right, Middle left, Middle right, Bottom left, Bottom right
  • Compass size: Small, Medium, Large (on-screen compass only)
  • Game bar text: Direction, Direction and degrees, Degrees
